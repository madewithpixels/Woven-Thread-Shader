Hero settings

Every thread in the hero above is drawn live on the graphics card, so nothing is loaded except the page itself. If the frame rate drops below the target, the hero lowers its resolution first, then the thread count, then the detail per thread, and steps back up when there’s headroom. The animation pauses when the hero leaves the screen. Visitors with “reduce motion” switched on see a still frame.
